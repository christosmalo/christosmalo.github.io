// 01 — Who I am

About Me

I am a PhD researcher at Sorbonne Université (CNRS · LIP6) in Paris, working on neuromorphic computing and FPGA-based Spiking Neural Network accelerators. I hold an Integrated Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (A.U.Th.), graduating in 2023.

My diploma thesis — awarded 10/10 — involved the design and construction of an Electrical Power System (EPS) for a nanosatellite. I have experience in PCB design, circuit simulation, and embedded systems, complemented by a strong background in programming and machine learning.
